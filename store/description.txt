Your day as an Old School RuneScape-style game screen, for the Pebble Time 2 and, new in this version, the Pebble Round 2 (beta).

- Big pixel clock and date
- Four status orbs: battery, heart rate, steps and sleep, or choose active minutes, distance or calories
- Live weather: temperature, today's high and low, the chance of rain or snow, sunrise and sunset
- Your real hiscores for Old School RuneScape or RuneScape 3: one skill, a different skill every few minutes, the skill closest to a level-up, XP gained today, a boss or clue kill count, or a Grand Exchange price watch
- Level-up moments, and a helm beside your name for Ironman, Hardcore and Ultimate accounts
- A chatbox with a rain countdown, steps left to your goal, XP to your next level and more
- Three themes: Classic stone, Dark, and Bright for sunlight
- On the Pebble Round 2: its own minimap layout with bigger text, and your XP to the next level as a ring round the minimap

Set everything up in Settings, with a live preview of the face. No accounts, no API keys, no ads. All art and fonts are original.

Round 2 owners: the round layout is built on the emulator, so please tell me how it looks on your wrist (GitHub link below).
